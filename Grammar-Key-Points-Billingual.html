1 · Finding Subjects and Verbs 搵主語同動詞

定義 Definitions

Verb = a word that shows action (runs, hits, slides) or state of being (is, are, was, were, am...)

動詞：表示動作（跑、打、滑）或狀態（is / are / was...）。

Subject = the noun or pronoun that performs the verb.

主語：做嗰個動作嘅名詞 / 代名詞。

Examples:
He ran around the block. / You are my friend. / The woman hurried.（woman 係主語）

搵主語 3 步

Rule 1: If a verb follows to, it is an infinitive phrase, not the main verb.

to + 動詞係不定式，唔係主要動詞。主要動詞喺佢前面或後面。

I like to walk. / The efforts to get her elected succeeded.

Rule 2: A subject will come before a phrase beginning with of.

主語喺 of 短語之前。唔好俾 of 後面嘅名詞呃咗。

A bouquet of yellow roses will lend color and fragrance to the room.

Rule 3: Always find the verb first. Then ask who / what performed the verb.

先搵動詞，再問「邊個 / 咩嘢做咗呢個動作？」嗰個就係主語。

The jet engine passed inspection. → Who passed? engine（jet 只係形容詞，唔係主語）
From the ceiling hung the chandelier. → What hung? chandelier，唔係 ceiling。

Rule 4: Any request / command such as “Stop!” or “Walk quickly.” has the understood subject you.

命令句省略咗嘅主語一定係 you。

(You) Please bring me some coffee.

Rule 5: Sentences often have more than one subject, more than one verb, or pairs of subjects and verbs.

一句可以有多個主語 / 動詞 / 主謂配對。

I like cake and he likes ice cream.（兩對）
He and I like cake.（兩個主語，一個動詞）
She lifts weights and jogs daily.（一個主語，兩個動詞）

2 · Subject and Verb Agreement 主謂一致

Basic Rule: A singular subject takes a singular verb, while a plural subject takes a plural verb.

基本規則：單數主語 + 單數動詞；複數主語 + 複數動詞。難在認清楚主語係單定複，同埋動詞邊個係單數。

記法：動詞加 -s 嗰個先係單數！He talks（單數）vs They talk（複數）。同名詞啱啱相反，名詞加 -s 係複數。
情況規則 Rule例句
or / nor 連接兩個單數主語用單數動詞My aunt or my uncle is arriving...
either/or, neither/nor + 兩個單數用單數動詞Neither Juan nor Carmen is available.
either/nor + 有 II 放第二，用 amNeither she nor I am going...
or / nor：單數 + 複數複數放後面，用複數動詞The bowl or the plates go on that shelf.
either/nor：單數 + 複數複數放後面，用複數動詞Neither Jenny nor the others are available.
and 連接兩個或以上主語一般用複數動詞A car and a bike are my means of transport.
each / every + 主語用單數動詞Each boy and girl has a book. / Every ... is...

易錯位：介詞短語 / 插入語唔影響主語

Look at the noun in the prepositional phrase only to decide singular/plural when the subject is one of: some, all, none, most, any, part, fraction etc.

主語係 some / all / none / most / any / part 等，要睇 of 後面嗰個名詞係單定複，先決定動詞。

Some of the pie is gone. / Some of the pies are gone.
None of the pie is gone. / None of the pies are gone.（none 可單可複，睇語境）

Collective nouns: Words like team, family, committee, group, crowd can be singular or plural depending on whether they act as one unit or as individuals.

集體名詞（team / family / committee）當一個整體用單數，當個別成員用複數。

The team is winning.（當一隊）/ The team are arguing among themselves.（各自）

Either / Neither as subjects: Always take singular verbs. Here / There: Subject follows the verb. Money / Time: Use singular verb.

either / neither 做主語永遠單數。Here / There 開頭，主語喺動詞後面。金額 / 時間當一個數目，用單數動詞。

Neither of them is available. / Either of us is capable.
There are four hurdles. / There is a high hurdle.
Ten dollars is a high price to pay. / Five years is a long time.

Who / That / Which as subject: Singular or plural according to the noun directly in front of them.

who / that / which 做主語，單複數跟前面嗰個名詞。

She is the one who is... / They are the ones who are...

Expressions: as well as, along with, together with, in addition to do NOT make a singular subject plural. The verb still agrees with the first subject.

as well as / along with / together with 唔會將單數主語變複數，動詞仍然跟第一個主語。

The teacher, along with her students, is coming.

3 · Pronouns 代名詞

A pronoun takes the place of a noun. It can be in one of three cases: Subject, Object, Possessive.

代名詞代替名詞，分三格：主格、受格（賓格）、所有格。

Case代名詞
Subject 主格I, you, he, she, it, we, they
Object 受格me, you, him, her, it, us, them
Possessive 所有格mine, yours, his, hers, its, ours, theirs（永遠唔加 ’）

Who vs. Whom

Rule: Use him/her method: if him/her fits, use whom; if he/she fits, use who.

代入法：代入 him / her 啱就用 whom；代入 he / she 啱就用 who。

Who / Whom did you call? → You called him. → Whom did you call? ✓
Who / Whom wrote this? → He wrote this. → Who wrote this? ✓

Whoever vs. Whomever

Look INSIDE the whoever/whomever clause. If the word is the subject inside the clause, use whoever; if object, use whomever.

睇 clause 入面：係主語用 whoever，係受詞用 whomever。

We will hire whoever is most qualified.（whoever 係 is 嘅主語）
Give it to whomever you choose.（whomever 係 you choose 嘅受詞）
Whoever is elected will serve... / Whomever you elect will serve...

Than / As 後面用邊個？

Mentally complete the sentence.

心入面補完整句先決定。

Zoe is taller than I (am). / Tranh is as smart as she (is).
Daniel would rather talk to her than me.（= than to me）vs than I（= than I would）意思完全唔同！

Reflexive 反身代名詞

myself, himself, herself, itself, ourselves, yourself, yourselves — use only when they refer back to another word in the sentence. Never use as a subject.

只可以用嚟指返句入面另一個字，唔可以自己做主語。

✗ Myself will do it. → ✓ I will do it myself.
✗ Give it to John and myself. → ✓ Give it to John and me.
its vs. it’s：its = 所有格（冇 ’）/ it’s = it is / it has（有 ’）。The dog wagged its tail. / It’s a cold morning.

That vs. Which

Who → people. That / Which → groups or things. That = essential clause（冇逗號）. Which = nonessential clause（有逗號包住）.

who 指人；that / which 指事物/團體。that 帶必要 clause（冇逗號）；which 帶非必要 clause（前後有逗號）。

I do not trust editorials that claim...（冇 that 就唔知邊篇，必要）
The editorial, which appeared on Sunday, upset me.（已經知邊篇，非必要，有逗號）

4 · Adjectives and Adverbs 形容詞 / 副詞

Adjectives describe nouns / pronouns. Adverbs describe verbs, adjectives, and other adverbs. Most adverbs answer how? and end in -ly.

形容詞修飾名詞/代名詞；副詞修飾動詞/形容詞/副詞。大部分副詞答「點樣？」同以 -ly 結尾。

問用例句
點樣？How?副詞 -lyShe speaks slowly.
咩樣？What kind?形容詞She is a slow speaker.

感官動詞特別規則

With taste, smell, look, feel — do NOT ask “how?” Ask: is the sense used actively?

taste / smell / look / feel 呢四個感官動詞，唔好問 how，要問「係咪主動用感官？」主動先加 -ly。

Roses smell sweet.（玫瑰唔會主動聞，唔加 -ly）
The woman looked angry.（形容外表，唔加 -ly）
The woman looked angrily at the paint.（主動望，加 -ly）
She feels bad about the news.（唔係用手摸，唔加 -ly）

Good vs. Well

Good = adjective. Well = adverb. Exception: health → use well.

good 係形容詞，well 係副詞。講健康要用 well。

You did a good job. / You did the job well.
You smell good today.（講氣味）/ You smell well for someone with a cold.（主動聞）
I do not feel well. / You do not look well today.（健康）
I feel good about my decision.（唔係講健康，可以用 good）

比較級 / 最高級

Compare 2 things → comparative (-er / more). Compare 3+ → superlative (-est / most). Never drop -ly when using an adjective in a comparison.

比 2 樣用比較級（-er / more）；比 3 樣或以上用最高級（-est / most）。比較時唔好甩咗 -ly。

She speaks more quickly than he does.（唔係 more quick）
Of the two, she is the better... / Of the three, she is the best...
These / Those：都係複數。These = 近處，Those = 遠處。These babies... / Those babies...

5 · Problems with Prepositions 介詞問題

Rule 1: You may end a sentence with a preposition. Just do not use EXTRA prepositions.

句尾可以用介詞，但唔好加多餘介詞。

✓ Where did he go? ✗ Where did he go to?
✓ Where did you get this? ✗ Where did you get this at?
✓ I will go later. ✗ I will go later on.
✓ Take your shoes off the bed. ✗ ... off of the bed.
✓ You may look out the window. ✗ ... out of the window.
✓ Cut it into small pieces. ✗ Cut it up into small pieces.

Rule 2: Use on with expressions that indicate the time of an occurrence.

講具體發生日子用 on。

He was born on December 23. / We will arrive on the fourth.

Rule 3: Of should never be used in place of have.

of 永遠唔可以代替 have。

✓ I should have done it. ✗ I should of done it.

Than vs. Then: Than = comparison. Then = when.

than = 比較；then = 時間（然後）。

I would rather go skiing than rock climbing. / First we went skiing; then we went rock climbing.

Like vs. As: Like should be followed by an object, not by a subject + verb. Use as for comparison with subject + verb.

like 後面跟受詞；as 後面可以跟主語 + 動詞做比較。

You look like your mother. / You look as your mother looked when she was young.（有主謂）

6 · Effective Writing 有效寫作

Rule 1: Use active voice whenever possible. Active voice means the subject is doing the action.

盡量用主動語態：主語就係做動作嗰個，句子更直接有力。

✗ The ball was hit by her. → ✓ She hit the ball.
✗ There are some revisions which must be made. → ✓ Please make some revisions.

Rule 2: Use concrete language rather than vague language.

用具體語言，唔好用空泛語言。

Rule 3: To make a strong point, use a positive statement rather than a double negative.

想講得有力，用肯定句，唔好用雙重否定。

✗ He is not unwilling to help. → ✓ He is willing to help.

Rule 4: Use similar grammatical form when offering several ideas. This is called parallel construction.

列舉幾個 idea 要用相同文法形式，叫平行結構。

✓ Check your spelling, grammar, and punctuation.（三個都係名詞）
✗ Check your spelling, grammar, and punctuating.

Rule 5: If you start a sentence with an action, place the actor immediately after — or you will create a dangling modifier.

句首係動作，緊接住要係做動作嗰個人，否則就係 dangling modifier（懸空修飾語）。

✗ While walking across the street, the bus hit her.（巴士行路？）
✓ While walking across the street, she was hit by a bus.

Rule 6: Place modifiers near the words they modify.

修飾語要放喺被修飾嘅字附近。

✗ I have some pound cake Mollie baked in my lunch bag.
✓ In my lunch bag, I have some pound cake that Mollie baked.

Rule 7: A sentence fragment occurs when you have only a phrase or weak clause but are missing a strong clause.

只有短語 / 弱句、冇主句，就係句子碎片（不完整句）。

✗ After the show ended. → ✓ After the show ended, we had coffee.
考試速記：主謂一致睇主語唔睇插入語 · who/whom 用 him 代入法 · that 必要冇逗號 / which 非必要有逗號 · good 形容詞 / well 副詞 · 比較用 than / 時間用 then · of ≠ have · 平行結構要同形 · 句首動作要跟返做動作嗰個人。
