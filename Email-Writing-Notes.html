1. 寫之前：Purpose 先行

Email 只有一個主要目的。 寫之前問：我寫嚟做咩？讀者睇完要做咩？唔好一個 email 講 5 件事。
  • Informing： 通知、更新
  • Enquiring： 問資料
  • Requesting： 要求行動 / 批准
  • Confirming： 確認安排
  • Complaining / Apologising： 投訴 / 道歉

對象 = 語氣：上司 / 客戶要正式啲；熟同事可以輕鬆啲，但仍要專業。唔好用 WhatsApp 口吻寫畀客戶。

2. 格式：Subject + Greeting + Body + Closing

部分要點例子
Subject line具體、講到重點、唔好只寫 Hi / HelpSeeking Approval for Safety Training Budget
Request for Meeting on 5 June
Greeting正式：Dear Mr Chan / Dear Ms Lee
較熟：Dear Raymond / Hi Raymond		唔知名：Dear Sir / Madam
Opening第一句講目的，唔好兜圈I am writing to seek your approval for...
Body一段一意、短句、重點用 list / 數字Background → Need → Solution → Benefit
Closing講下一步 + 有禮收尾I look forward to hearing from you.
Sign-off正式：Regards / Kind regards
更正式：Yours sincerely		唔好用 Cheers / Best 畀唔熟嘅客戶
常見衰位：Subject 太空泛、冇附件但寫咗 attached、冇講 deadline 但期望人哋即刻覆、成段冇分段。

3. 語氣 + 時態：禮貌唔等於長

Modal Verbs：禮貌程度

功能由直接 → 最禮貌
要求Please send... → Could you please...? → Would you mind...? → I would be grateful if you could...
建議You should... (較強) → I recommend... → You could... / May I suggest... (較軟)
申請I would like to seek your approval for...

時態：唔好亂轉

  • 講已發生：Past Tense — We received a complaint this morning.
  • 講現在情況：Present Tense — The current projector is over 8 years old.
  • 講將來行動：Future / Modal — We will arrange... / I would appreciate it if you could...
  • 附件：The proposal is attached. / I have attached... for your review.

課堂重點：同一段入面唔好無啦啦轉時態。寫緊「已經做咗咩」就全段用 Past；寫緊「建議」就用 would / should。

4. 好 Email vs 壞 Email

✅ 好❌ 唔好
Subject 具體Subject 空泛 / 冇 subject
開頭講目的開頭寒暄三段都未入正題
短句、分段、list一嚿大段文字、冇分段
數字、日期、金額寫清楚「盡快」「大概」「唔知幾多」
禮貌但直接過份客套、官腔、兜圈
講明下一步 + deadline冇講要人做咩、幾時覆
壞例子 (p.11 上半段嗰類)：
成段好長、冇分段、講咗好多背景但冇講要人做咩。
改寫方向：第一句講目的 → 分段講 Background / 問題 → 講你嘅建議 → 講要對方幾時覆 + 附件。

5. 講壞消息：點講先唔得罪人？

  • 唔好直接用負面字：Error, will not, unavailable, never, impossible, reject, unacceptable, bad, poor, misunderstanding, mistake, failure, refuse — 盡量避。
  • 用 linking words 連接壞消息 + 原因：because / as / since
  • 技巧：先講原因，後講壞消息，感覺冇咁硬。
  • 唔好淨係講「公司政策」：要講點解有呢個政策，否則等於冇解釋。
❌ It is our company policy to settle all invoices within 30 days.
✅ In order to comply with ISO standards, we need to have consistent payment terms with all vendors.

❌ Unfortunately, we are unable to provide you with our fees.
✅ Unfortunately, until we have a better understanding of the work you are seeking, we are unable to provide you with our fees and an approximate total cost.
→ 先講原因 (until we have...)，後講結果，聽落合理好多。

常用壞消息句式

We regret that we are unable to... / In the best interests of our customers, we do not normally... / Unfortunately, data is required to... / We regret to inform you that we are unable to... / Regrettably, we are unable to...

6. Case-Study One：回覆查詢 / 要求資料

結構：Acknowledge → Answer / Provide → Closing

  1. Para 1 — Acknowledge：多謝對方 email / 查詢。
    Thank you for your e-mail regarding...
  2. Para 2 — Answer：直接答，唔好兜圈。提供資料 / 解釋流程 / 講下一步。
  3. Para 3 — Closing：有禮收尾 + 聯絡方法。
    If you have any questions, please feel free to contact me at...

課堂例子：回覆 Ms Wong 問「點樣續牌 / 點樣申請」→ 先講多謝查詢 → 講明步驟 1-2-3 → 講埋要交咩文件、幾時前交 → 收尾留電話。

7. Case-Study Two：推卻請求 / Turning Down

結構：Acknowledge → Politely turn down + Reason → Suggestion / Compromise → Polite Close

  1. Para 1 — Acknowledge：Thank you for your e-mail regarding the delivery time extension.
  2. Para 2 — Turn down + Reason：用 because / as / since 連接，唔好只講「唔得」。
    We regret that we are unable to... because...
  3. Para 3 — Suggestion / Compromise：盡量畀個替代方案，顯示你有心幫。
    However, you could consider contacting... / May I suggest that you... / However, we would be able to accept 20 days payment instead.
  4. Para 4 — Polite Close：I hope that this is acceptable. / I am sorry again that we could not help this time. / Please let me know if this is acceptable to you.

8. Case-Study Three：升級畀管理層 / Escalating

幾時要 Escalate？需要建議 / 需要批准先做到 / 已經解決咗但要通知上司。

結構：Purpose → Summary → Actions Taken → Proposed Solution / Request + Deadline → Closing

  1. Para 1 — Purpose statement：第一句講明要咩。
    I am writing to seek your advice regarding... / I am writing to seek your approval for... / I am writing to inform you of an issue which occurred this morning.
  2. Para 2 — Brief summary：只講讀者需要知先做到決定嘅重點，句子短。用 1-2 段講晒，太長要再分段。
  3. Para 3 — Actions already taken：講咗已做咩、成功定唔成功，等上司唔會重複建議同一樣嘢。
    Janice's supervisor, Leo, has already called the customer to apologise but it was not accepted...
  4. Para 4 — Proposed solution + Deadline：禮貌咁講你嘅建議 + 要對方幾時覆。
    I am wondering if it would be possible for you to draft the formal apology letter... The customer requested a response by the end of the week, so would you mind returning to me by the end of tomorrow?
  5. Para 5 — Closing：Thank you for your help in advance. / I look forward to receiving your reply / comments / feedback.

9. Case-Study Four：申請批准 / Seeking Approval

黃金結構：Purpose → Background → Need → Detail / Solution → Benefit → Attachment → Request + Closing

例子：Safety Training Budget ($500,000)

段內容
PurposeI would like to seek your approval of additional funding of $500,000 for providing Safety Training courses for our staff.
Background隧道個人受傷平均每月 6 人，遠高於目標每月少於 1 人。
NeedTherefore, we have been urgently requested by our Safety Committee to provide additional training.
Detail / Solution已取得報價：2-day course，$100,000 / 50人，250人共 $500,000。
Benefit完成課程可獲 Grade A，更重要係員工更 aware 危險，意外數字有望大減至低於目標。
AttachmentThe proposal with a detailed cost breakdown is attached.
Request + ClosingTherefore, it would be appreciated if you could approve the budget so that training can be organized next month. / I look forward to hearing from you soon.

另外兩個例子：同一個結構

  • 租多個倉 (Sample One)：現有倉只存到 5 年文件，但按《稅務條例》要存至少 7 年 → 建議租多個倉 → 地點葵芳、月租 HK$4,500、300 sq ft → 實際只需 150 sq ft 存 1 年文件 → 求批准。
  • 買新 Projector (Sample Two)：舊機 8 年、成日壞、投訴多、維修完都係差 → 建議買新機，功能更多 → 已問 3 間供應商報價，詳情 attached → 希望 8 月下一輪 training 前裝好。

11. Send 之前 Checklist ✅

  • ☐ Subject 具體？讀者一眼知咩事？
  • ☐ 第一句已講目的？
  • ☐ 一段一意？有分段 / list？
  • ☐ 數字、日期、金額、附件寫清楚？
  • ☐ 語氣啱對象？唔好太串 / 太官腔？
  • ☐ 時態一致？
  • ☐ 壞消息有冇原因 + 替代方案？有冇避開負面字？
  • ☐ 有冇講下一步 + deadline？
  • ☐ 附件真係 attach 咗？收件人啱唔啱？
  • ☐ 讀一次：對方會唔會誤會？
