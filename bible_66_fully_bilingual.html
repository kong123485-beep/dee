📖 聖經 66 卷書中英雙語對照

新舊約全書中英文書名與完整中英內容解說

📜 舊約聖經 (Old Testament, 39卷)

1. 摩西五經 / 律法書 (The Torah / Law, 5卷)

記載世界的起源、以色列列祖的故事以及上帝頒布的律法與立約。
Records the origins of the world, the stories of the patriarchs, and God's law and covenant.
創世記(Genesis)
記載上帝創造天地萬物、人類始祖墮落、全球洪水審判，以及揀選亞伯拉罕一家成為以色列列祖的歷史起源。
Details the creation of the universe, human fall, global flood, and the historical origins of choosing Abraham's family as the patriarchs of Israel.
出埃及記(Exodus)
記述以色列人在埃及為奴，神藉由摩西和神蹟帶領他們出離埃及，並在西奈山頒布十誡與律法、建造會幕。
Describes the Israelites' slavery in Egypt, God leading them out through Moses and miracles, giving the Ten Commandments at Sinai, and building the Tabernacle.
利未記(Leviticus)
詳細規範祭司的獻祭制度、潔淨條例、節期與聖潔生活的標準，教導罪人如何親近至聖的上帝。
Details sacrificial systems, cleansing regulations, festivals, and holy living standards for priests, teaching sinners how to approach a holy God.
民數記(Numbers)
記錄曠野中的人口清查、百姓的多次叛逆與神的管教，導致以色列人在曠野漂流四十年的過程。
Records wilderness censuses, repeated rebellions and divine discipline, leading to the 40-year wandering of Israelites in the desert.
申命記(Deuteronomy)
摩西在進入應許之地前夕，對新一代以色列人重申律法、十誡，並呼籲他們堅守與神的約定。
Moses' final farewell to the new generation before entering the Promised Land, reiterating the law, Ten Commandments, and covenant loyalty.

2. 歷史書 (Historical Books, 12卷)

以色列人進入應許之地、建立王國、分裂、亡國被擄到回歸重建的歷史軌跡。
The historical trajectory from entering the Promised Land, monarchy, division, exile, to return and reconstruction.
約書亞記(Joshua)
在約書亞領導下，以色列人渡過約旦河，征服迦南地並按支派抽籤分配土地的得勝歷史。
Under Joshua, Israelites crossed the Jordan, conquered Canaan, and triumphantly distributed the land by tribal lots.
士師記(Judges)
描述以色列人屢次離棄神、受外族欺壓，神便興起士師拯救，陷入「離棄—受苦—呼求—拯救」的惡性循環。
Depicts a cycle of apostasy, oppression, crying out, and deliverance through judges when Israel repeatedly forsook God.
路得記(Ruth)
士師時代背景下的溫馨外邦女子故事，突顯神的忠實慈愛，並串聯出大衛王與耶穌的家譜血脈。
A heartwarming story set in the judges' era about a foreign woman, highlighting God's loyal love and tracing David's genealogy.
撒母耳記上(1 Samuel)
先知撒母耳崛起、士師時代落幕，以色列要求立王，掃羅受膏為首任君王卻因不順服被棄，大衛隨之興起。
Samuel rises, judges era ends, Saul becomes first king but is rejected for disobedience, and David emerges.
撒母耳記下(2 Samuel)
大衛王作王統一以色列、定都耶路撒冷，經歷國力鼎盛與因罪跌倒的痛苦，展現神對悔改之人的恩典。
David unifies Israel, establishes Jerusalem as capital, experiences peak power and tragic sin, showcasing God's grace to the repentant.
列王紀上(1 Kings)
所羅門繼位建殿、晚年離棄神導致王國分裂為南北（以色列與猶大），以及先知以利亞對抗偶像崇拜。
Solomon builds the temple; later apostasy splits the kingdom into North and South; Elijah fights idolatry.
列王紀下(2 Kings)
記載南北兩國持續腐敗的歷史，北國因罪先被亞述帝國滅亡，南國隨後也被巴比倫攻陷並被擄至異地。
Chronicles the decline; the Northern Kingdom falls to Assyria, and the Southern Kingdom is later captured by Babylon.
歷代志上(1 Chronicles)
從亞當的家譜追溯至大衛王，重點聚焦於大衛為建造聖殿所做的一切屬靈與物質預備。
Traces genealogy from Adam to David, focusing heavily on David's spiritual and material preparations for the temple.
歷代志下(2 Chronicles)
從所羅門建殿開始，回顧猶大諸王的興衰，直至耶路撒冷被毀、百姓被擄，以及波斯王下詔回歸。
From Solomon's temple to Judah's kings, Jerusalem's destruction, exile, and Cyrus' decree allowing return.
以斯拉記(Ezra)
猶大人在波斯時期分批回歸耶路撒冷，在以斯拉帶領下重建聖殿並推動屬靈的認罪與改革。
Jews return in waves during the Persian era under Ezra to rebuild the temple and drive spiritual reform.
尼希米記(Nehemiah)
尼希米心繫故鄉，回國帶領百姓克服阻撓、齊心協力在極短時間內修復重建耶路撒冷城牆。
Nehemiah returns to lead the people in overcoming opposition to swiftly rebuild the walls of Jerusalem.
以斯帖記(Esther)
被擄期間，猶大女子以斯帖冒死拯救同胞免遭滅族災難，彰顯神在隱密中的保守與普珥節的由來。
During exile, Esther risks her life to save her people from genocide, showing God's hidden preservation and the origin of Purim.

3. 詩歌與智慧書 (Wisdom & Poetry, 5卷)

涵蓋人類深層的情感、苦難的思索、敬拜的讚美以及實用的處世智慧。
Covers deep human emotions, reflection on suffering, worship, and practical living wisdom.
約伯記(Job)
探討義人無辜受苦的深奧哲理，透過約伯的苦難與上帝最後的回應，挑戰世俗因果報應的狹隘觀念。
Explores the profound mystery of innocent suffering, challenging simplistic karmic retribution through Job's trials and God's response.
詩篇(Psalms)
古代以色列人的讚美詩與禱告總集，涵蓋讚美、感恩、哀傷、懺悔與信靠，是心靈的最佳安慰。
A collection of ancient Israeli hymns and prayers covering praise, thanksgiving, lament, repentance, and trust.
箴言(Proverbs)
所羅門等人的處世智慧格言，教導人如何以敬畏耶和華為首，活出正直、勤奮與公義的智慧人生。
Practical maxims teaching moral living, diligence, and righteousness, rooted in the fear of the Lord.
傳道書(Ecclesiastes)
深刻反思日光之下世事皆空、虛空幻滅，最終指出人當盡的本分是敬畏上帝並遵守祂的誡命。
Reflects on the vanity of life under the sun, concluding that fearing God and keeping His commandments is humanity's true duty.
雅歌(Song of Solomon)
以優美的抒情詩歌頌婚姻中愛情的純真、美好與忠貞，象徵神與屬靈子民之間親密的愛。
A lyrical poetry celebrating romantic love and marital fidelity, symbolizing the intimate relationship between God and His people.

4. 先知書 (The Prophets, 17卷)

傳達神對罪惡的審判、對回頭者的應許，以及對彌賽亞降臨的預言。
Conveys God's judgment on sin, promises of restoration, and prophecies of the Messiah.
以賽亞書(Isaiah)
被譽為舊約的福音書，嚴厲斥責罪惡，並充滿大量關於彌賽亞降生、受苦僕人與新天新地的宏大預言。
Known as the Gospel of the Old Testament, featuring grand prophecies of the Messiah's birth, the suffering servant, and a new creation.
耶利米書(Jeremiah)
流淚先知呼籲猶大人遠離罪惡、接受巴比倫的審判，並預言未來神將與人立「新約」。
The weeping prophet warns Judah to repent, submits to Babylonian judgment, and prophesies a future New Covenant.
耶利米哀歌(Lamentations)
先知對耶路撒冷淪陷與聖殿被毀的哀悼與痛悔，在極深痛苦中仍仰望神的慈愛與誠實。
A mournful dirge over the fall of Jerusalem and the temple, yet looking to God's steadfast love and faithfulness amidst deep grief.
以西結書(Ezekiel)
被擄時期的先知記載枯骨復活等震撼異象，宣告神審判罪惡，但也預言榮耀將重回未來新聖殿。
Exilic visions like the valley of dry bones; announces judgment but promises God's glory returning to a future restored temple.
但以理書(Daniel)
記錄被擄宮廷的忠心見證、獅子坑神蹟，以及透過末世君王與帝國興衰異象給予百姓盼望。
Courageous exilic court accounts, the lions' den miracle, and apocalyptic visions of future empires providing hope.
何西阿書(Hosea)
以先知娶不忠之妻的痛苦經歷，痛切比喻以色列對神的不忠與神那永不止息的烈火之愛。
Uses the prophet's painful marriage to an unfaithful wife as a metaphor for Israel's unfaithfulness and God's passionate love.
約珥書(Joel)
藉蝗災警告大審判將臨，呼籲全國禁食悔改，並應許末世必將聖靈澆灌凡有血氣的。
Uses a locust plague to warn of coming judgment, calls for national repentance, and promises the future outpouring of the Spirit.
阿摩司書(Amos)
牧人出身的先知嚴厲譴責社會富豪欺壓貧窮與虛假的宗教慶典，宣告神的公義如大水滾滾。
A shepherd prophet fiercely denounces social injustice and empty ritualism, declaring that justice should roll down like waters.
俄巴底亞書(Obadiah)
舊約最短的書，嚴詞警告以東因幸災樂禍與驕傲，將在神的審判中被徹底除滅。
The shortest book in the Old Testament, warning Edom of utter destruction due to arrogance and gloating over Judah's downfall.
約拿書(Jonah)
不願順服的先知被大魚吞吃，最終無奈前往死敵尼尼微傳道，彰顯神對外邦人的憐憫與大愛。
A reluctant prophet swallowed by a great fish goes to Nineveh, highlighting God's vast mercy and compassion for all nations.
彌迦書(Micah)
責備領袖與社會的貪婪腐敗，並精準預言未來的彌賽亞將降生於卑微的小鎮伯利恆。
Rebukes corrupt leaders and greed, and precisely prophesies that the future Messiah will be born in humble Bethlehem.
那鴻書(Nahum)
宣告殘暴不可一世的亞述帝國首都尼尼微將徹底滅亡，安慰受欺壓的猶大人：神是公義的避難所。
Declares the utter downfall of Nineveh, the brutal capital of Assyria, comforting Judah with God as a refuge.
哈巴谷書(Habakkuk)
先知對神為何容許惡人橫行提出靈魂拷問，神回答「義人必因信得生」，帶領他從困惑轉為歡欣信靠。
The prophet questions why God allows evil; God replies that the righteous shall live by faith, transforming doubt into trust.
西番雅書(Zephaniah)
嚴厲警告耶和華大而可畏的日子將臨到列國與犯罪的猶大，但末後必留下謙卑的餘民得享復興。
Warns of the dreadful Day of the Lord coming upon nations and Judah, but promises a humble remnant will be restored.
哈該書(Haggai)
鼓勵剛從巴比倫回歸、生活困頓的百姓放下自私，優先修繕荒涼的聖殿，神必大施祝福。
Encourages returned exiles to prioritize rebuilding the ruined temple over personal comfort, promising divine blessing.
撒迦利亞書(Zechariah)
透過異象鼓勵重建聖殿，並預言彌賽亞將騎驢進城、被賣受傷，最終建立永恆的君王國度。
Visions encouraging temple rebuilding, with rich prophecies about the Messiah riding a donkey, being pierced, and reigning.
瑪拉基書(Malachi)
舊約最後一卷書，斥責百姓什一奉獻不誠與信仰冷淡，預告在彌賽亞來臨前必有先知以利亞先期重臨。
The final Old Testament book, rebuking spiritual apathy and tithing issues, predicting Elijah's return before the Messiah.
📖 新約聖經 (New Testament, 27卷)

1. 四福音書 (Gospels, 4卷)

紀錄耶穌基督的生平、言行、神蹟、受死與復活。
Records the life, teachings, miracles, death, and resurrection of Jesus Christ.
馬太福音(Matthew)
寫給猶太人的福音，證明耶穌就是舊約所預言的彌賽亞、大衛的子孫，強調天國的王權與律法精義。
Written for Jews to prove Jesus is the promised Messiah and Son of David, emphasizing the kingdom of heaven.
馬可福音(Mark)
節奏明快、充滿行動力，凸顯耶穌是那位服事人、為世人捨命的「受苦僕人」與神的大能權柄。
Fast-paced and action-oriented, portraying Jesus as the suffering Servant who gives His life as a ransom.
路加福音(Luke)
由外邦醫生路加撰寫，詳細追溯歷史，特別強調耶穌對窮人、女性、罪人與外邦人的極大憐憫與救恩。
Written by the Gentile doctor Luke, emphasizing Jesus' immense compassion for the poor, outcasts, women, and Gentiles.
約翰福音(John)
充滿深奧神學與七個神蹟，直指耶穌是永恆的神子（太初有道），強調信靠祂即可獲得永恆的生命。
Filled with profound theology and seven signs, showing Jesus is the eternal Son of God (the Word), granting eternal life to believers.

2. 歷史書 (Historical Book, 1卷)

記載早期教會的建立與福音的廣傳。
Records the establishment of the early church and the expansion of the gospel.
使徒行傳(Acts)
記載耶穌升天後，聖靈降臨，使徒們如何在迫害中將福音從耶路撒冷傳到猶太全地、撒馬利亞，直到地極羅馬。
Traces the spread of the gospel from Jerusalem through Judea and Samaria to Rome under the power of the Holy Spirit.

3. 保羅書信 (Pauline Epistles, 13卷)

使徒保羅寫給各教會與同工的真理教導、信仰勸勉與牧養。
Truth instructions, exhortations, and pastoral care written by the Apostle Paul.
羅馬書(Romans)
保羅最偉大的神學巨著，系統闡述「因信稱義」、罪的權勢、律法的有限與基督裡神豐富的恩典。
Paul's masterwork systematically explaining justification by faith, the power of sin, and God's abundant grace in Christ.
哥林多前書(1 Corinthians)
針對哥林多教會內部紛爭、道德淪喪、恩賜運用與復活真理等混亂現象提出嚴厲的矯正與教導。
Corrects church divisions, moral failure, abuse of spiritual gifts, and addresses the doctrine of bodily resurrection.
哥林多後書(2 Corinthians)
保羅流露真情的自白書，辯護自己使徒的權柄，並勉勵信徒在苦難中經歷主的安慰與樂捐的恩典。
Paul's emotional defense of his apostolic authority, encouraging believers to find divine comfort in suffering and give generously.
加拉太書(Galatians)
強烈駁斥靠行割禮或律法才能得救的異端，重申基督徒是因信稱義、在聖靈裡得享真正的自由。
Strongly refutes legalism, reaffirming that believers are justified by faith alone and enjoy true freedom in the Spirit.
以弗所書(Ephesians)
闡明教會是基督的身體、信徒在基督裡同為後嗣的屬靈豐盛，並教導基督徒應當穿戴屬靈軍裝站立得穩。
Explains the church as Christ's body and believers' spiritual wealth in Christ, urging them to put on the armor of God.
腓立比書(Philippians)
保羅在監獄中寫下的喜樂之書，勉勵信徒效法基督謙卑捨己的樣式，無論順逆皆靠主常常喜樂。
Paul's epistle of joy written from prison, encouraging believers to imitate Christ's humility and rejoice in the Lord always.
歌羅西書(Colossians)
高舉基督是萬有的首生者、神一切豐盛都有形有體居住在祂裡面，駁斥當時的哲學與異端迷惑。
Exalts Christ's supremacy over all creation and fullness of deity, warning against worldly philosophies and heresies.
帖撒羅尼迦前書(1 Thessalonians)
安慰和鼓勵在患難中的帖撒羅尼迦教會，勸勉聖潔度日，並詳細闡述主耶穌再來的真理。
Encourages a persecuted young church to live holy lives, providing clear teaching on the return (rapture) of Jesus.
帖撒羅尼迦後書(2 Thessalonians)
矯正信徒因誤以為主立刻再來而荒廢工作的錯誤觀念，提醒眾人必須提防大罪人顯現。
Corrects misunderstandings regarding the timing of Christ's return and warns against idleness and the man of lawlessness.
提摩太前書(1 Timothy)
保羅寫給年輕同工提摩太的牧養指導，教導如何防範異端、設立教會長老執事與管理教會秩序。
Pastoral guidance to Timothy on guarding against false doctrines, appointing church leaders, and maintaining order.
提摩太後書(2 Timothy)
保羅殉道前的最後遺囑，極其感人，勉勵提摩太在苦難中忍耐、勇敢傳道並持守純正真理。
Paul's final emotional testament before martyrdom, urging Timothy to endure hardship, preach the word, and guard truth.
提多書(Titus)
指導在克里特島服事的提多如何建立健全的教會，強調純正的信仰必須帶出榮神益人的美好善行。
Instructions to Titus on organizing healthy churches in Crete, emphasizing that sound doctrine must produce good deeds.
腓利門書(Philemon)
一封短小溫馨的私人求情信，求主人腓利門饒恕並接納逃跑的奴隸阿尼西謀，彰顯基督裡的饒恕與弟兄愛。
A brief personal appeal to Philemon to forgive and welcome back his runaway slave Onesimus as a beloved brother in Christ.

4. 普通 / 大公書信 (General Epistles, 8卷)

其他使徒與領袖寫給全體信徒的指引與勸勉。
Guidelines and exhortations written by other apostles and leaders to believers at large.
希伯來書(Hebrews)
深入論證耶穌的祭司職分與中保地位遠超舊約的摩西與天使，勸勉信徒在逼迫中堅忍到底。
Argues that Jesus' priesthood and covenant surpass Moses, angels, and old sacrifices, urging believers to persevere.
雅各書(James)
耶穌的弟弟雅各所寫，強調「沒有行為的信心是死的」，教導信徒如何勒住舌頭、關懷貧窮與實踐信仰。
Emphasizes that faith without works is dead, teaching practical wisdom on taming the tongue and caring for the poor.
彼得前書(1 Peter)
鼓勵散居各處、飽受苦難逼迫的基督徒，提醒他們有尊貴的君尊祭司身份，效法基督受苦留下的榜樣。
Encourages scattered and persecuted believers, reminding them of their royal priesthood and Christ's example in suffering.
彼得後書(2 Peter)
警告信徒提防假教師與異端邪說的迷惑，堅定應許主必再來，勸勉信徒在恩典與知識上不斷長進。
Warns against false teachers, affirms the certainty of Christ's return, and urges growth in grace and knowledge.
約翰一書(1 John)
使徒約翰晚年所寫，強調神就是光也是愛，教導信徒當彼此相愛、行義，藉此驗證自己擁有永生。
Emphasizes that God is light and love, calling believers to love one another and practice righteousness to verify eternal life.
約翰二書(2 John)
短小的警惕信，提醒信徒要緊守基督的愛與真理，切勿接待傳異端邪說的假導師進家門。
A short warning to walk in truth and love, and refuse hospitality to false teachers spreading heresy.
約翰三書(3 John)
稱讚慷慨接待傳道人的該猶，同時嚴厲指責貪權、惡意毀謗人的丟特腓，勉勵信徒效法善行。
Praises Gaius for hospitalizing traveling missionaries while rebuking the power-hungry Diotrephes.
猶大書(Jude)
主耶穌的弟弟猶大所寫，嚴厲斥責混入教會的惡劣異端與不虔誠的人，勉勵信徒在真理中造就自己。
Warns against ungodly intruders who crept into the church, urging believers to build themselves up in the faith.

5. 預言書 (Prophecy, 1卷)

關於世界歷史終局與救恩完成的奧祕啟示。
Mysterious revelations concerning the end of world history and the fulfillment of salvation.
啟示錄(Revelation)
新約唯一的一卷預言啟示書。以豐富神秘的象徵異象，描繪世界歷史終局、撒但的潰敗、基督的凱旋與新天新地的永恆榮耀。
The New Testament's only prophetic book, using symbolic visions to depict the final defeat of Satan, Christ's triumph, and the New Heaven and Earth.
