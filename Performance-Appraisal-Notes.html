1. 點解 Appraisal 咁難寫？

核心矛盾：要誠實評價 + 要令人接受 + 要符合行政程序 + 要留低書面紀錄，會影響升遷。
  • 主觀 vs 客觀：印象 ≠ 證據。要用可觀察、可量度嘅事實。
  • 對事唔對人：評價 工作表現，唔係評價性格、人品。
  • 寫畀多人睇：當事人、上司、審核委員會、將來查閱嘅人。語氣要專業、持平。
  • 中英思維差異：中文含蓄、重關係；英文評核要直接、具體、講到重點。

2. SMART：寫工作目標

寫畀下屬嘅工作目標，一定要過到 SMART 呢關。

字母意思問自己
SSpecific 具體做咩？邊個範疇？清唔清楚？
MMeasurable 可量度點知做得好唔好？有冇數字 / 標準？
AAchievable 可達到資源、時間、人手做唔做到？
RRelevant 相關同部門 / 職位目標有冇關？
TTime-bound 有期限幾時完成？每月幾多次？
MC Q1 答案 C —— 點解最 SMART？
❌ A) To design and facilitate in-house workshops — 冇數量、冇標準、冇時間
❌ B) ...approximately 10 per month — 有數量，但冇質素標準
✅ C) To carefully design and engagingly facilitate approximately 10 in-house workshops per month achieving an average of 4 out of 5, or higher, on End-of-Workshop Evaluation
→ 有做咩 (design + facilitate)、有幾多 (10/month)、有質素標準 (4/5)、有量度方法 (Evaluation)

3. 3-P Approach：寫平衡評核

MC Q4 答案 B：Person / Practice / Product

唔好只集中講性格 (Person)。三樣要平衡：

3-P寫咩例子方向
Person 人態度、合作、溝通、責任感willing to help, works well with colleagues
Practice 做事方法工作方法、程序、準確度、時間管理follows procedures, checks work, meets deadlines
Product 成果完成咗咩、質素、影響completed X reports, error rate reduced, service improved
常見錯誤：成篇只講「勤力、友善、有責任心」→ 冇 Product，睇唔到實際貢獻；或者只講成果 → 睇唔到方法有冇問題。

4. 結構：點樣組織一份 Assessment

  1. 開頭：職責簡介
    講明期內主要職責係咩，唔好直接跳入評價。寫職責時用 過去式 / 完成式，因為係已做嘅工作。
  2. 主體：按範疇 / 按目標分段
    每個範疇：表現 + 證據 + 例子。唔好將讚同彈撈埋一堆。
  3. 改善空間：講具體行為 + 建議做法，唔好只講「要改善」。
  4. 總結：整體評級
    總結要同前面內容一致，唔好前面彈、結尾突然讚。
記法：職責 → 表現 (證據) → 改善 → 總結
每一段開頭用 Topic Sentence，例如：Mr So performed satisfactorily in handling store vouchers... 之後先講證據。

5. Substantiation & Evidence：講咩都要有據

  • 唔好得個講字：「表現良好」→ 點好？幾多次？幾準？有咩結果？
  • 用數字 + 具體事例：完成咗幾多份、幾時前完成、錯誤率、投訴數字、客戶回饋。
  • 用可觀察行為：❌ He is careless. → ✅ He submitted three reports late and with calculation errors in...
  • 比較要公平：唔好同唔同職級 / 唔同職責嘅人直接比較。
課堂例子 — Ms Chan (Original vs Rewritten)：
Original：寫佢「有做嘢」但冇講佢點做好，仲有重複、語氣唔一致。
Rewritten 學到：
① 職責寫得清：responsible for general office administration, covering filing, procurement and personnel matters
② 表現用證據：She completed all assignments on time and maintained accurate records...
③ 改善講具體：She could further improve by... 而唔係人身攻擊

6. 評級詞彙：用字要同評級一致

MC Q2 答案 C：Effective Performance

題目句：Mr Chan demonstrated a familiarity with statutory requirements and was able to complete his work on time and in-line with departmental procedures.

點解係 Effective 而唔係 Outstanding / Very Effective？因為只係講「識、做到、跟程序、準時」→ 達到要求，但冇講超越要求、創新、帶嚟重大影響。

評級 (由高到低)應該用咩詞？
Outstandingexceptional, consistently exceeded, outstanding contribution, exemplary
Very Effectiveconsistently exceeded expectations, highly effective, strong impact
Effectivemet requirements, competent, reliable, completed on time, in line with procedures
Moderategenerally satisfactory, with some areas needing improvement, inconsistent at times
Unsatisfactory / Poordid not meet requirements, frequently late, repeated errors, despite guidance
考試陷阱：用字唔好「升」或「跌」咗個評級。寫 Effective 但用 Outstanding 嘅詞，審核嗰陣會被質疑；寫 Outstanding 但只寫「做到」就唔夠說服力。

常用正向詞彙（要配證據）

conscientious, diligent, reliable, proactive, meticulous, efficient, cooperative, responsive, sound judgement, good communication skills

講改善嘅溫和但準確寫法

needs to pay closer attention to detail / could benefit from... / should further develop... / would benefit from more supervision in...

7. 語言準確：最常見文法錯

MC Q3 答案 E：All of the above

錯誤例子 / 點改
A. Missing articles (a, an, the)❌ He is responsible officer → ✅ He is the responsible officer
B. Singular vs plural noun❌ three report → ✅ three reports；❌ one of the staffs → ✅ one of the staff / members
C. Wrong / missing prepositions❌ responsible to handle → ✅ responsible for handling
❌ familiar on → ✅ familiar with
D. Wrong tense寫已完成工作要用 Past Tense：❌ He is completing... → ✅ He completed...

其他要留意

  • 主謂一致：He / She + verb-s；The team was... vs members were...
  • 唔好中式英文：直接譯中文句子，好易變咗冗長、主語唔清。
  • Proofread：交之前逐句讀一次，特別睇 articles、prepositions、tense。

8. Tone：點樣講改善先令人接受？

MC Q5 答案 D：All of the above

  • A. Focus on solution, not problem：唔好只講「你錯咗」，要講「下次可以點做」。
  • B. 用 Passive 減輕指責感：適當用被動語態，唔好句句「You failed...」
  • C. Constructive + actionable advice：建議要具體、可做到、有期限。例如：He should double-check figures before submission.
唔好咁寫：
❌ He is lazy and careless. He always makes mistakes.
→ 性格標籤 + 絕對化 (always)，冇證據、令人反感。

要咁寫：
✅ During the reporting period, three reports were submitted late with calculation errors. He has been advised to check his work more carefully and to seek clarification when in doubt. With closer attention to detail, his performance should improve.

Tone Checklist

  • 公平、持平、基於事實
  • 唔用情緒化、誇張、絕對化字眼 (always / never / totally)
  • 唔用性格標籤 (lazy, stupid, difficult)
  • 批評要配建議，讚要配證據

9. 改寫練習：Mr So 例子點解咁差？

Original Assessment 問題：

  • 職責段太長、撈埋晒：一句過講晒 vouchers、uniform、 medals、computer system，讀者唔知邊樣最重要。
  • 評價空泛：「General speaking, he is willing... However he has been advised to pay more attention...」→ 到底係邊方面唔專心？幾多次？有咩後果？
  • 前後矛盾 / 唔一致：一邊話要改善，一邊又話「relationship has been improved」→ 改善咗咩？點證明？
  • 總結太求其：「On the whole, acceptable」→ 同前面內容對唔上，冇講點解係 acceptable 而唔係其他評級。
Rewritten 方向（考試咁寫）：
① 先分職責：Mr So was responsible for [A], [B] and [C] during the reporting period.
② 每個職責講表現 + 證據：He handled X vouchers / processed Y requests accurately and on time...
③ 改善要具體：He was advised to pay closer attention to... For example, [具體事件]. He should...
④ 總結要回應前面：Overall, his performance was acceptable. He met basic requirements in..., but needs to improve... to reach effective performance.

10. Workshop Review MC — 答案 + 一句原因

題答案原因
1. SMARTC有具體工作 + 數量 + 質素標準 (4/5) + 量度方法
2. 評級詞彙C「做到、準時、跟程序」= Effective，唔係 Outstanding
3. 文法錯EA-D 全部都係常見錯：articles / 單複數 / 介詞 / 時態
4. 3-PBPerson / Practice / Product
5. 正面回饋D講解決方案 + 被動減指責 + 具體可行建議，全部都要
